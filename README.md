# Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

Seasonal Agriculture Performance Analysis is a data analytics project that studies agricultural performance across different seasons, crops, irrigation methods, and states.

The project uses Python-based data analysis and visualization techniques to identify patterns in crop yield, water efficiency, revenue, cost, and profit.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Analyze agricultural performance across different seasons.
- Compare crop yields across Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid seasons.
- Study water usage and efficiency for different irrigation methods.
- Compare agricultural profitability across states.
- Identify useful patterns and insights from agricultural data.
- Present findings through clear visualizations.

## 📊 Dataset

The dataset contains **4,000 farm records and 28 columns**.

It includes information about:

- Farm and crop details
- Season and state
- Farm area
- Rainfall and temperature
- Humidity and sunlight
- Soil conditions
- Fertilizer and pesticide usage
- Crop yield and production
- Market price
- Cost, revenue, and profit
- Water usage and water efficiency
- Disease and pest risk

## 🛠️ Technologies Used

- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Jupyter Notebook

## 🔍 Analysis Performed

### 1. Seasonal Analysis
Analyzed the distribution and performance of Kharif, Rabi, and Zaid seasons.

### 2. Crop Analysis
Compared the average yield of different crops across seasons.

### 3. Irrigation Analysis
Compared irrigation methods based on water usage, water efficiency, and average yield.

### 4. State-wise Analysis
Compared states based on average profit, revenue, cost, yield, and profit margin.

### 5. Statistical Analysis
Used descriptive statistics and correlation analysis to understand relationships between important agricultural variables.

## 📈 Key Findings

- Kharif has the largest share of records at **44.5%**.
- Rabi accounts for **40.7%** of the records.
- Zaid accounts for **14.8%** of the records.
- Kharif shows the highest average yield and profit among the three seasons.
- Sugarcane has the highest average yield among the analyzed crops.
- Water efficiency differs across irrigation methods.
- Punjab and Maharashtra have the highest average agricultural profits in the dataset.
- Yield and water efficiency show a strong positive association in the dataset.
